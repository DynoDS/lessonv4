# Working-wall visual review

Use this file only when `ARTEFACT` is `working wall`.

**One card, one page, with two exceptions.** `working-wall.json` declares how many cards the wall earns and what size each is. A card that overflows onto a second sheet is a fault even when both sheets are individually tidy, because a teacher pins one card to a wall: a second sheet holding two stray lines is either lost or pinned beside the first to show four short steps. The exceptions are a `banner` and a `mnemonicPoster`, which are built to run across several sheets, one word or one letter each, and are pinned end to end.

**Numbered steps beside their steps.** Where a card numbers its steps, each number has to sit against the first line of the step it labels. A number vertically centred against its whole wrapped block appears, from across the room, to number a different line. This is the kind of fault that only shows in the render, so read the numbers against the lines rather than assuming the order is right.

**Readable from across the room.** A wall card is read from further away than a slide and stays up for weeks. Apply the legibility check above at that distance, and treat a text panel wrapping every three or four words as a fault: it means the panel was given far less width than the page has spare, which is the blocking "room to enlarge sits unused" case.

### Optional decoration

When P3 is present, check relevance, subordination and non-obstruction. Covering
meaningful text, a task, answer/workspace, label or figure is BLOCKING. Harmless
cosmetic awkwardness is MINOR at most. Missing P3 is never a fault. P3 has no
cross-resource carry-across requirement.

Ignore P3 when checking whether the card has an honest meaningful visual. P3
cannot rescue a words-only card.
