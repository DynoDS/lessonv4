This is a walk-through of a primary lesson, written slide by slide.

PASS if, for each part of the lesson where children do something, it is clear what the child has to work out. The thought may be stated outright or be obvious from the task itself.

FAIL if parts of the lesson describe only an activity with no thinking behind it, such as discussing with a partner, completing a stem, or copying from the board, where a child could finish by finding the words already on the slide.

FAIL if the document is a list of activities with reasons attached rather than a lesson written in the order a class meets it.

Judge the substance, not the formatting or the length.
