This is a walk-through of a Year 4 science lesson on the types of human teeth and their uses.

PASS if children are asked to explain or predict something about teeth that they could not answer by reading a label. For example: working out why a tooth's shape suits the job it does, deciding which tooth an animal or a food would need and saying why, or explaining what would go wrong if a tooth of one type had to do another type's job.

FAIL if the whole lesson is naming, labelling, matching or copying. Being shown the four types, labelling a mouth diagram, matching names to pictures, and sorting cards by name are all naming tasks, however many of them there are.

FAIL if the only explaining happens in a single closing question tacked on at the end.

Judge the substance of what children do, not the wording or the formatting of the document.
