Year 4, English.
Learning objective: To punctuate direct speech using inverted commas.
Episode: about 25 minutes, from the first teaching to the end check.
Authorised learning: inverted commas go around the words actually spoken; the punctuation that ends the spoken words (a comma, question mark or exclamation mark) goes inside the closing inverted comma; a reporting clause such as "said Sam" follows, with a comma after the spoken words when the sentence carries on.
Out of scope today: starting a new line for a new speaker, and reporting clauses placed before the speech.
Prior knowledge: capital letters, full stops, question marks and exclamation marks; children know speech bubbles.
Supplied text you may use (unpunctuated, from the class story):
I can't find my shoe said Leo
Have you looked under the bed asked Mum
It's not there shouted Leo
Class: a mixed Year 4 class of 30.
Teacher's note: Speech bubbles are fine; I want them to see that inverted commas do the same job as the bubble.
