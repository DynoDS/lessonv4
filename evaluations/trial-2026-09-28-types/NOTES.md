## Pattern across today's reviews
Four lessons were sent back for the same kind of fault: a Do whose answer is already on the board (PSHE twice, Codex geography, phones). Worth one upstream fix in the designer. I tested a mechanical check (star fact words appearing in the Do's model answer) over every design from 27-28 September: it would fire on dozens of lessons the reviewer rightly approved, because a star fact beside a writing task is often a proper reference. So no check was added; the reviewer is catching these. It is a judgement for Daniel: should a Do slide ever show the star fact the Do is about?
