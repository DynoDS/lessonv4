# Teacher review: Year 4 Geography Lesson 1 (2 November 2026)

Deck: Autumn 2, Week 1, Geography, built on 4.2.335. Working folder `working/year-4-geography-lesson-1-where-in-the-world-are-tropical-rainforests`. Daniel's verdict, summarised from his message:

- "A weak lesson in general. It's confusing. A cover teacher couldn't pick it up. The wording's wrong. A lot of the actual visuals are wrong or in the wrong place." Too long.
- **Dos are fine.** Four new places is strong, what a geographer would do. Abdi is fine (a map used as a geographer would) but too texty, with nothing to help anyone below greater depth.
- Slide 1: "Which continent is each number on?" feels off.
- Slide 3 (UK wood, "flying south"): irrelevant in the moment, teaches nothing, a weak teacher would not draw the right questions from it.
- Slide 5 (finger on the UK, follow the line, landing place): "this isn't teaching at all". If the point is where the Equator is from the UK, teach that.
- Slide 6: says Equator and Africa with neither shown. Wanted: teach what and where the Equator is, a quick Do, then the rainforest there.
- Slide 8: the first actual teaching, and not very effective.
- Slide 9: "a forest like this" with no forest on the slide; should show rainforest with information, the desert, then the question.
- "We've spent 10 slides wasted." "Where's my explaining? Where's the making a point, the because so, linking the misconception that it's not this but it's this?"
- Slide 12: biomes taught on one slide, not effectively; pictures too small; what is the difference between them?
- Slide 13: map full of letters with no meaning until slide 14; "Geographers use the weather..." random and abstract.
- Slide 15: relies on remembering A is Asia and D is Oceania; "landing place" is not geographical; "What do you notice" hopes a greater depth child sees it, with nothing to help.
- Slide 16: liked: big statement, the because, "more like my explaining". But "where we landed" is no longer on the map, and the directions are hard to follow.
- Slide 17: says what it is, not much teaching.
- Slide 18: border line worded oddly and helps nobody.
- Slide 20: no link from Abdi to Describe the Amazon.

**His general rule:** each slide should work as if there were no other slides; what it refers to must be on it.
