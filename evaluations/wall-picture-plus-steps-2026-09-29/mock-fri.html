Tens, then ones
1
Partition the second number into tens and ones.
25 = 20 + 5
2
Count on the tens from the first number.
48 + 20 = 68
3
Will the ones go past the next ten? If not, add them.
68 + 5 goes past 70
4
If they will, add enough ones to reach the next ten.
68 + 2 = 70
5
Add the ones that are left.
70 + 3 = 73
