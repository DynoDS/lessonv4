Year 4, Science, 45 minutes.
Learning objective: To explain how sound travels from a source to our ears.
Prior learning: children have made sounds with instruments and know that sounds are made when something vibrates.
Class: a mixed Year 4 class of 30.
Teacher's note: They need to come away knowing the vibration travels through the air, not that the sound just goes.
