Year 4, History, 45 minutes.
Learning objective: To explain why the Vikings came to Britain.
Prior learning: children know where Scandinavia and Britain are on a map, and that the Anglo-Saxons lived in Britain before the Vikings arrived.
Class: a mixed Year 4 class of 30; three children read below their age.
Teacher's note: I want them to understand there was more than one reason, and that raiding and settling are different.
