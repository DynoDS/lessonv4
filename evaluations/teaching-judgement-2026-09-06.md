# Lesson teaching judgement repair

## Intended result

Design lessons around what children encounter and understand, the teacher's concrete explanation or demonstration, and a manageable opportunity to use the learning. Preserve the approved objective, necessary knowledge, meaningful independent work and Daniel's natural teaching voice. A correct structure and answerable questions alone are insufficient.

## Evidence and cause

The rejected history run used the updated 4.2.102+codex.20260906163614 package and read the relevant designer, history, preferences and voice instructions. The designer explicitly rejected a modern comparison image because written summaries supplied the facts. It grouped school access, apprenticeship and modern education into one chunk. The final reviewer approved both pedagogy and Daniel-fit, repairing only one timeline sentence. The supplied plan suggested a contract but did not require the added teaching route; the designer's own notes recognised that detailed Tudor work belonged in Lesson 2.

The producing faults were decisions about teaching medium, manageable learning and source relevance. The review amplified them by accepting broad structural features as evidence of teacher fit. This is not evidence of a failed installation or image retrieval.

The existing worker-setting test follows that change. No new production agent, reference file, schema field, validator or review stage is introduced. Evaluation material remains outside runtime guidance.
