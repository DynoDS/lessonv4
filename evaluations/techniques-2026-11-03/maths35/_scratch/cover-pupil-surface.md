## Vocabulary

whole: The whole is the full amount, everything put together.
part: A part is one bit of the whole. Two parts added together make the whole.

## What does a bar model show?

This is a bar model. It shows a number and the two parts it's made of.
The long bar on top is the whole: 4,000. Underneath, the same length is split into two parts: 2,500 and 1,500.
2,500 and 1,500 make 4,000 together. So 4,000 take away 2,500 leaves 1,500, just like in the starter.
The whole is 4,000 and one part is 1,500. What is the other part?
