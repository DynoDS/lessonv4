Indigenous peoples: Indigenous peoples are the groups of people whose families were the first to live in a place, long before anyone else arrived.

## Why build a village by the river?

Look where this village is: right on the bank of the river.
The river gives them water to drink, to cook with and to wash in.
It's full of fish to catch and eat.
There are no roads through most of the forest, because the trees grow too thick. So the river is their road, for canoes and small boats.

## What does the forest give them?

The forest gives Indigenous peoples their homes, much of their food and their medicine.
Look at this house. The posts are tree trunks, and the roof is made of big palm leaves woven together, so the rain runs off.
Brazil nut trees grow right up into the emergent layer. Their hard pods fall to the forest floor, and people collect the nuts inside.
When someone is ill, there's no chemist nearby. Over thousands of years, Indigenous peoples have learned which plants help, and they make medicine from them.
