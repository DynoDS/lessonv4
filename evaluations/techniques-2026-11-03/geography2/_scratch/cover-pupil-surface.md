## Vocabulary

vegetation: Vegetation means all the plants that grow in a place.
canopy: The canopy is the thick roof of leaves where the treetops touch.
emergent: Emergent trees are the few giant trees that poke out above the canopy.

## What's at the top of the rainforest?

On a sunny day, it's shady under a big tree.
Its leaves stop the sunlight reaching you.
In a rainforest, the vegetation, all the plants, grows in layers, like the floors of a tall building.
At the top, the trees grow so close that their leaves join up into a thick roof, the canopy. Most animals live up here, where the fruit grows in the sun.
A few even taller trees poke out above the canopy. These are the emergent trees.
Why is it shady under the tree?
Like the tree in the park, the canopy catches the sunlight first.

## Vocabulary

understorey: The understorey is the layer just under the canopy, where young trees grow.

## What is it like under the canopy?

The canopy catches nearly all the sunlight, so the lower you go, the darker it gets.
Under the canopy is the understorey. Young trees grow here in dim light, with huge leaves to catch what light gets through.
At the very bottom is the forest floor. It's the darkest place of all, even at midday.
So hardly any plants grow there. The ground is covered in dead leaves.
A rainforest isn't the same all the way down. Each layer gets a different amount of light.
What do you think it's like under the canopy?

## What happens when a giant tree falls?

Sometimes a giant rainforest tree gets old and crashes down.
When it falls, it leaves a big hole in the canopy, like a hole in a roof.
This happens all the time in a rainforest.

## What is it like in the canopy and on the forest floor?

What is it like in the canopy and on the forest floor, and why?
The canopy gets lots of sunlight because it is near the top. So the vegetation is thick and green, and most animals live there.

The forest floor gets hardly any sunlight because the canopy catches it first. So the vegetation is thin, with only a few small plants.
The canopy is green and has lots of animals. The forest floor is dark and has dead leaves.
