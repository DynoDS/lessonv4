# Techniques, not blanket rules: test lessons (3 November 2026)

The ten rules Daniel settled on 2 and 3 November (`evaluations/do-develops-2026-11-02/rules-followed-word-for-word.md`) were built into the working copy of the plugin, uncommitted and not installed. Each folder here is a lesson designer run on that working copy, with the real inputs of a lesson he has already had: Year 4 Geography lesson 1, Year 4 Maths lesson 35 and Year 4 History lesson 6. Only the designer ran. There was no review, voice edit, pictures or slides, so these are lesson plans to read, not decks.

| Lesson | Check | Honest time | What stood out |
|---|---|---|---|
| geography1 | passed | about 49 | Teaches what and where the Equator is and why it is hot there, shows a park, a desert and a rainforest before the word biome, and has no pretend flight. Its first draft timed at about 54, and it trimmed board lines to fit. |
| maths35 | passed | about 50 | One modelled example per cycle, against two before; all the independent goes are kept. The version taught from scored 56. |
| history6 | passed | about 52 | Kept at 52 with a line to the teacher saying why, which is the new backstop working. Its first draft was 59 beats and was refused, so it cut boards back and moved fuller telling into the scripts. |

**What this does not show.** These are three runs, nothing was taught, and the slide designer's new duty to read each slide alone was not exercised. Two of the three designers still drafted long and then trimmed, so the early read for feel did not stop that. Read their boards (`_scratch/cover-pupil-surface.md` in each folder) to judge whether what they trimmed was a line that could go or teaching that should have stayed.
