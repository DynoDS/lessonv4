## Vocabulary

biome: A biome is a big area of the world with its own kind of weather, and the plants and animals that suit it.

## What decides what can grow in a place?

The weather in a place decides what plants can grow there.
Here in Britain, summers are warm, winters are cold, and it rains on lots of days. Oak trees grow well here.
In a hot desert, it hardly ever rains, so only a few tough plants can grow.
In a tropical rainforest, it's hot and rainy all year, so huge trees grow close together and stay green.
A big area like this, with its own kind of weather and the plants and animals that suit it, is called a biome.
Why can't a forest like this grow in the desert?

## Vocabulary

Equator: The Equator is an imaginary line around the middle of the Earth, halfway between the North Pole and the South Pole.

## Where do rainforests grow?

The green on this map shows where tropical rainforests grow.
Near the Equator, the Sun shines strongly all year, so it's hot every month.
The heat lifts water up into the air, and it falls again as heavy rain, nearly every day.
Further from the Equator, the Sun's rays arrive at a slant, so it's cooler.
What do you notice about where they are?
Tropical rainforests grow near the Equator, where it is hot and rainy all year.

## Where is the Amazon rainforest?

The Amazon is the biggest tropical rainforest in the world.
It's in South America. Most of it is in Brazil, but it spreads into other countries too, like Peru and Colombia.
The Equator runs right through it, so it's hot and rainy all year.
The Amazon River winds through the middle. The forest is more than 20 times bigger than the whole of the UK.
