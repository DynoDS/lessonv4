Card kits for Why did Tudor children work: teacher notes. Keep this away from the pupil pages.

Kit Deal: Which parts of the deal would a Tudor family have minded losing most?
Unit: lesson-section/teaching-sequence/unit-006
Prepare: At tables, one set between two, after slide 9 (the deal). (15 sets printed, one between two).
Children are told: Put each card under a heading. Then choose the two cards a Tudor family would have minded losing most.
Headings: Helped the family straight away | Helped the child when he grew up
Answer:
  a hot dinner every day -> Helped the family straight away
  one less mouth to feed at home -> Helped the family straight away
  knowing when bread is baked just right -> Helped the child when he grew up
  his own bakery one day -> Helped the child when he grew up
  a straw mattress by the oven -> Helped the family straight away
  a warm coat for winter -> Helped the family straight away
Also accept: The baking card under either heading: he learned it now, and it earned his living later. The two the family minded losing most: knowing when bread is baked just right (the trade) and one less mouth to feed.
