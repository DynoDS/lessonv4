Kit Tom's deal: Help now or help later?
Unit: lesson-section/teaching-sequence/unit-004
Prepare: At tables, one set between two, straight after the deal slide. (15 sets printed, one between two).
Children are told: Put each card under a heading. Then pick the card that was hardest to place, and write why you put it there.
Headings: Helped him straight away | Helped him when he grew up
Answer:
  a hot dinner every day -> Helped him straight away
  knowing when bread is baked just right -> Helped him when he grew up
  a straw mattress by the oven -> Helped him straight away
  his own bakery one day -> Helped him when he grew up
  a warm coat for winter -> Helped him straight away
Also accept: Accept knowing when bread is baked just right under either heading when the reason says Tom was learning it now or would use it to earn a living later.
