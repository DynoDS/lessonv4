# Levels trial, 30 September 2026 (late)

Change under test: props as the prepared-day level planned whenever the best task uses them (chosen for the thinking, never the props); levels are as many as a task sensibly has; tasks as an educational break; picture cards and sheets printable.

| Lesson | Expected | What the designer did |
|---|---|---|
| Conductors (testing needs kit) | board demo + prepared-day kit, no invented print | exactly that: teacher tests at the front with one circuit; prepared day, pairs test at tables; "No printed version: two lists of letters in a book are the whole record"; safety lines added |
| Roman artefacts (all three) | replicas / printed photo sheet / board | board only: "Nothing needs preparing: everything runs from the slides"; hands-on never considered in the record |
| Human and physical (two) | picture sheet or cards + board | picture-card kit in pairs (six photos, three traps); prepare line "Print and cut one set..." with no board fallback named |

Findings: the rule lands when the task is plainly practical (testing) and when a sort is the task; it is skipped when the evidence is pictures on the board (artefacts); the board fallback is missing from the prepare line in 2 of the last 4 kit lessons (Nativity handson, features).
