# Does the lesson repair transfer beyond history?

6 September 2026. Tested installed Lesson v4 `4.2.102+codex.20260906183853`, corresponding to repair commit `d2d7acd5d65284c1157faf6dfce6653f3fcb16f7`.

## The target

Daniel wants a coherent lesson he can open and teach: an intelligible route, worthwhile learning, concrete explanation, useful visible support, natural wording, vocabulary where it helps, and child work that uses the teaching. Simple does not mean empty slides, trivial thinking, or the same structure in every subject. The supplied plan supplies objective, coverage and sequence; its suggested activities do not override pedagogical judgement.

These are shared design and review criteria, with subject and structure guidance selected as needed. The visual profile also applies to slide production, but that downstream stage was not exercised here.

## Method

The protocol and stopping rule were recorded before outputs. Three fresh designers used the installed package at its normal Astra/medium setting, with no conversation inheritance, no history complaint and no evaluation criteria in their prompts. Three independent normal Astra/high reviews examined the resulting lessons. All first drafts were retained. At most one normal repair and fresh review was budgeted per rejected case.

These are evaluator-authored test commissions based on Daniel's requested topics, not quotations from him. Year 4 and 45 minutes were used. The geography plan contains three lesson-3 entries; this test uses the first, Who lives in the rainforest? The other two (cocoa and rivers) were not tested. Its supplied objective is To describe how Indigenous peoples use the Amazon rainforest. Adjacent lessons were read. The two PSHE objectives deliberately change the required learning while keeping the topic constant.

## Observed results

| Case | Actual teaching | Review outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geography: describe how Indigenous peoples use the Amazon rainforest | Contemporary community, river and garden photo contracts; links from resources to food, journeys, homes and medicines; one clearly labelled imagined forest-loss diagram; immediate use followed by independent descriptions and a new consequence | Approved after two bounded wording corrections, including removal of an advance judgement in the ending |
| PSHE: describe a range of jobs and explain how strengths and interests can be useful in work | Four concrete occupations and their tasks; examples connect abilities and interests to the work; individual explanations and a fresh same-job/different-strength case | First draft rejected for Daniel-fit: strengths and interests were explained before a Do using strengths only. Repaired into separate Teach/Do pairs with vocabulary at each point of use; fresh review approved after two bounded wording corrections |
| PSHE: discuss what might matter when choosing a future job and explain a personal view | Fictional offers make pay and enjoyment concrete; working hours are then revealed; children revisit advice, compare real contributions and explain individual priorities | Approved without corrections; different defensible conclusions remain acceptable |

All three first designs passed schema and review preflight. All four review records, including the initial rejection and the fresh recheck, passed postflight. Those checks establish valid contracts and review records, not teaching quality.

## What this establishes

The deployed system has demonstrated transfer beyond the history case and discrimination between different learning demands within one topic. It did not force historical source comparisons or photographs into PSHE. Vocabulary was placed at relevant teaching moments. The reviewer caught a new sequencing problem and two wording problems instead of approving every plausible design.

It has not demonstrated perfect first drafts: one of three required semantic repair, and another required bounded corrections. Nor are three deliberately chosen cases a random sample, a reliability percentage, or a controlled baseline comparison. The reviewers' Daniel-fit judgements apply documented preferences; they are not Daniel's endorsement of these particular lessons.

This is a design/review test. No images were acquired and no PowerPoint, worksheet layout or full lesson pack was rendered. It cannot prove the final slides' simplicity or physical usability. No new topic-specific production rule is justified by these results alone; the existing review-and-repair path is being exercised.

## Final decision and launch verification

All three current designs were approved by the normal review process. No production instructions or model settings were changed in this transfer check. The existing package caught and repaired the observed faults; the new evidence does not justify adding a careers or rainforest rule. Retain these cases for future regression work, including the legitimate text-based discussion case where extra photographs would not establish the choices being weighed.

All eight workers in this transfer check matched their declared settings. The task-wide audit marker is `WORKER_LAUNCH_AUDIT_FAILED: 2 of 24 named workers did not launch at their declared model and effort`, because it includes the two deliberately different reviewer configurations from the earlier controlled investigation. Those historical controls are not mismatches in these eight new launches.

Local evidence is in `work/transfer-trials`: protocol, unmodified first drafts under each case's `before-review`, full canonical designs, initial PSHE rejection, final reviews, gate receipts and worker audit. These remain design-stage evidence, not a delivered resource pack or teacher endorsement.
