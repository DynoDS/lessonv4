One row per paragraph, bullet or code block, so nothing in the three files is outside the list.
The quote is the paragraph's opening words, exactly; `check-ledger-quotes.py` finds each one.
A row with no fate is a rule kept word for word. Fates: **home** (the one copy kept), **fold** (removed
here, any extra condition carried to the home), **story** (dates off, his words kept, the incident a
plain example or a reason), **trim** (maintainer or history text out), **move**, **fix**, **ask**
(a decision for him), and x:S2 / x:S3 (a copy outside these files, settled in that piece).
