# Lesson amount and practical Do beats: the plan (30 September 2026)

Written after the Year 4 RE Nativity lesson "lost the class" ("a lot of listening, a lot of sitting ... they came away listening, not learning") and a day of test lessons. Evidence lives in `evaluations/trial-2026-09-30-childseat`, `trial-2026-09-30-amount` and `trial-2026-09-30-quickdo`, each with a NOTES.md.

## What is already built (uncommitted, not in Codex)

- The child's-seat timeline (`scripts/do-beats-in-a-row.py`, also printed in the review view): every listening stretch, every task, alone or together, cards or board, planned minutes, and the count between the starter and the main work.
- `preferences.md` → `Use variety deliberately`: changing the answer format is not variety when every task is done alone, off the board, handing back what was just said; why/because is not the default Do.
- `preferences.md` → `A lesson holds two or three Dos, then its final task`: two sizes of Do (two or three counted tasks; uncounted one-minute quick checks that make children think); never squeeze, drop or bundle to fit.
- Apply counts toward the amount; the retelling's lines group into two or three chunks.
- Tested on seven designs: the count held every time, teacher-only talk fell, bundling seen once (before the anti-bundling line), two designers chose a quick check to break long teaching on their own.

## The direction

Do the least that makes the next real lesson better, in this order. Each step is judged before the next starts.

### Step 1. Test the reviewer (release waits)

His ruling, 30 September 2026: nothing is released or installed while this is being tested; when he is happy he pushes it all to GitHub himself. So the only part of the old Step 1 kept here is one design-reviewer run on a trial design (the sound lesson), folded into Step 2's testing, to see whether it reads the child's-seat section and the amount rule.

### Step 2. Practical Do beats, in three levels (guidance)

His rule: every practical task has a board version that works with no preparation; a printed version the plugin makes is the upgrade; real materials appear only when he has said he has them. Chosen for the learning, never to alternate ("not a balance just because the first Do beat wasn't practical").

1. `do-beats.md`: a new family, "Hands-on", with a handful of entries (sort or order picture cards; stack or order printed pieces; move counters on a printed map or timeline; handle printed photos of artefacts or specimens; a quick test at tables; make a simple model). Each says what it is best for, when the board does the same thinking (so the board wins), its board version, its printed version, and what real materials would add.
2. `preferences.md` → `Use variety deliberately`: the one choosing question, "would holding it or doing it make children think better than seeing it on the board?", with the reasons that say yes (they can change their mind by moving it, testing gives real evidence, a model shows something hidden) and the named mistake of alternating.
3. `lesson-designer.md`: plan the board version first; one "prepare" line in the teacher orientation giving the three levels in ten seconds of reading; real materials only when the brief or plan names them.
4. Subject files: add the practical forms each subject really uses to `What a Do beat looks like`.

Done when: rerunning the test lessons puts a practical task where it clearly helps (a science test, story pictures to order) and leaves it out where it does not, with the designer's reason written down.

### Step 3. Make the printed versions real (engine)

1. Picture cards in the card kit (his decision 18, 23 September, never built): a sort or an order whose items carry pictures prints them.
2. Printed pieces beyond cards, only if Step 2's trials keep asking for them (strips to stack, a map with cut-out counters).
3. The sort layout that could not fit five two-line headings (Nativity slide 13).

Done when: a built lesson prints a picture-card kit that matches its board version, and the checks pass.

### Step 4. Check it in a real classroom

He teaches one lesson made on the new version and says whether the class stayed with him.
