1. Four-quadrant coordinate grid (the waiting one, installed, and its numbers made readable from a seat).
2. Long multiplication on the board and wall, built up one move a slide. Worksheets now print the same drawing.

4. Fraction wall with a ruler line down it and the family shaded.
5. Ten frame under its own name, with counters, two colours, a pair for numbers to 20.
6. A cycle diagram (water cycle, life cycles, the blood's loop, two loops through the heart).
7. Clock with only the long hand or only the short hand, and coloured hands without the digital time.

**Two were already built**, so I left them: exchange marks on column subtraction (10 October) and squared bar chart axes (9 October).

**One is not a drawing:** the whole river from source to mouth. A river's course is a real place. Since your ruling of 12 October a picture can be made when no photograph is found, and that is the right route for it.
