# Design reviewer rule ledger (streamline topic 8, first list)

Step 1 of the streamline method in `streamline-plan.md`, for one topic: the
independent review of a finished lesson design. Nothing in the plugin was
changed to make it.

**Status.** Built overnight, 23 to 24 September 2026, and checked by an
independent agent on 24 September (`streamline-tools/design-reviewer-inventory-check.md`).
Every verified finding is in: 18 new rows, corrected facts, strengths and
duplicates, and the decisions reshaped into nine settled items and two
questions. Nothing is marked unverified: every finding was confirmed in the
files. No decision has been answered yet.

**Snapshot.** The plugin's working tree on the night of 23 September 2026:
lesson-v4 4.2.287 (commit `79426973`) with the success-criteria change
(4.2.288) uncommitted on top. Quotes are taken from that working tree, not from
a commit, and line numbers are the line in the working tree where the quote
starts. Every «quoted» passage is the file's own words;
`streamline-tools/check-ledger-quotes.py` checks each one.

**What counts as the design reviewer here.** Everything the plugin says about
the independent review of a finished lesson design: the reviewer's own file
whole, its focused-repair file and the route-checks file; what it reads and
when (its reading list, the sections it reads every review, the line below
which a route file is not read); what it may correct itself and what it must
return to the lesson designer, and how the hand-back and the repair work; what
the review packet program prints for it (the routing card, the review view with
`As the class meets it`, the names list, each Do beside its teaching, the cues)
and what it checks afterwards; what the validator has refused before the
review starts; the review's place in the run (the playbook's Phase 1.25, shared
with the playbook topic); and your boundaries for it (the reviewer is the one
net, no caps, "too much" is a judgement against Pride Lessons and never a
number, the final resource review retired on 13 September), wherever they are
written.

**What belongs to a neighbouring topic.** Every earlier topic put its own
checks into the reviewer's file: vocabulary (`VOC-`), assumed knowledge
(`AK-`), the Teach then Do rhythm (`TD-`), quick checks (`QC-`), success
criteria (`SC-`) and worksheets (`WS-`). Those lines have rows here marked
"shared" with the owning topic and its row ID, because this list's job is the
reviewer as a whole; the first five topics pinned them, so their wording moves
only with those pins. The routes, subject files and voice guide are the other
topic 8 lists (`RT-`, `SJ-`, `VG-`); the playbook is topic 10 (`PB-`); the rest
of `preferences.md` is topic 7 (`PF-`); the slide, worksheet, wall and
adaptation designers are topic 9.

## How to read a row

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | `RV-` then a group letter and a number. Groups: A what the review is for and your boundaries round it, B the material-defect boundary and the two judgements, C User-fit: amount, returns and whether each Teach board teaches, D the four outcomes and what is not polish, E trusting the validator and what it refused first, F what the reviewer reads and when, G the voice sweep, H the learning contract, I route, modelling and independence (with the route checks), J thinking, practice and evidence, K language, load and teacher usability, L the worksheet (shared with worksheets), M sources, scenarios and pictures, N the consistency sweep and the drift check, O what the reviewer may correct and what it must return, P the report, Q the focused repair, R the review's place in the run (shared with the playbook), S what the review packet prints and checks, T what other files say about the review. |
| What the agent is told | The rule's own words. Several «quotes» in one row are separate sentences of the same rule. |
| When it applies | The condition, and any exception the text gives. |
| Strength | **must** (never, always, only, or refused by code), **default** (normally, usually, prefer), **may** (a permission), **check** (a test the reviewer runs), **mechanics** (how to record, read or run it). "Code" means a program refuses or prints it. |
| Code names | Field names, markers, headings, messages or commands a program or test depends on. These cannot change without the code. |
| Where | The file, its section and line. The primary row of a rule lists its copies. |
| Kind | rule; your example; your ruling; duplicate; near-duplicate (with what it adds or lacks); contradiction (a numbered decision below); story (a dated incident); pointer; maintainer; stale; code; shared (another topic owns it, named with its row ID). |
| Proposed home | A proposal only. **REV** the reviewer's own file, as the reviewer's check; **REV-ROUTE** the route-checks file; **REV-REPAIR** the focused-repair file; **CARD**, **VIEW**, **VERIFY** the review packet's routing card, review view and after-review check (code); **PB** the playbook (topic 10); **PREF** `preferences.md`, section named; **LD** the lesson designer's own file; **ROUTE**, **SUBJ**, **TV** a route file, a subject file, the voice guide; **LOG** `build-review-log.md`; **CUT** a duplicate that folds into the row named; **STAYS** stays where it is because another topic owns it. |
