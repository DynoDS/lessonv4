## The topic

Everything the plugin says about a lesson's success criteria: what they are for (a live reference children use while they work, not a label); when a lesson has them and when it does not; how many steps and how each step reads (one sentence per step, no explanation the teaching already gave, in words the child already owns, no word the lesson coined for something visible treated as owned); the form following the task; where the criteria appear (the Teach and Do slides, the practice slide, the worksheet, the stick-in pack, the working wall) and what each surface does with them; building criteria live with the class and `drawLive`; criteria that continue a previous lesson's steps word for word; the colour marks on criteria words (picture, taught, decide), including the teacher's vocabulary decision 16, parked here: "the success criteria should color any vocab words in green. But this is different... if there's something that is actually important, one or two words that are important, but they're not quite vocab, those can be coloured" (every taught word green in criteria; the one-or-two limit is for the other marks); the panel's size on a slide (at most half a slide, except a slide that is only the criteria); the reviewer's checks on criteria; and what the programs check or print about criteria (`validate-lesson-design.py`, `design-review-packet.py`, `lesson-design-scaffold.py`, any builder that renders the panel).
