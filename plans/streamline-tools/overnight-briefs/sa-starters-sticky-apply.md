## The topic

Everything the plugin says about how a lesson opens and closes, and what it leaves children with:

- **The starter:** what it is for, what it may ask (recall of earlier learning, a hook, a prerequisite check), its formats and timing, when it reaches back to earlier lessons or the plan, how it is shown and answered, what the reviewer checks about it, and what the programs check or scaffold (`starter` in `lesson-design.json`).
- **Sticky knowledge:** what counts as the lesson's lasting takeaway, how it is worded, how many, where it appears (slides, the wall, stick-ins) and how it is referenced (`stickyKnowledge`, `stickyKnowledgeRefs`).
- **The Apply slide** and **practising a test question** (preferences' own sections and every copy). The teacher removed test-question starters twice (20 and 21 September 2026) and does not want them rebuilt; list what remains and any trace of them, marked as such.
- **Purposeful endings and linked lessons:** the ending beat (`ending`), plenaries, exit questions, what a lesson hands to the next, and the "Purposeful Endings and Linked Lessons" section.
