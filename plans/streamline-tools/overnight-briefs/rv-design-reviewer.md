## The topic

Everything the plugin says about the independent review of a finished lesson design:

- `agents/design-reviewer.md` whole, `agents/design-reviewer-focused-repair.md`, and `references/design-review-route-checks.md`;
- what the reviewer reads and when (its reading list, the sections it reads every review, the line below which a route file is not read);
- what it may correct itself and what it must return to the lesson designer (bounded corrections, REDESIGN REQUIRED), and how the hand-back and repair work;
- what `scripts/design-review-packet.py` prints for it (the review view, "As the class meets it", the cues and triggers), and what the validator has already refused before it runs;
- the review's place in the run (the playbook's Phase 1.25, shared with topic 10);
- the teacher's boundaries for it (the design reviewer is the one net, no caps, "too much" is a judgement against Pride Lessons never a number; the final resource review retired 13 September), wherever they are written.

Every earlier topic put its own reviewer lines into the reviewer's file. Those lines get rows here marked shared with their topic (with the row ID from that topic's ledger), because this list's job is the reviewer as a whole: what it is for, how it works, and what is repeated or contradicted inside it.
