### Your 23 September ruling

Asked on 23 September whether a sheet used away from the board should write what
a child needs into its questions (success criteria, "After the first change

- "no overcomplications. worksheets are not homework, worksheets are delivered

So every sheet is done in class, inside the lesson, with the board and the

### His answers, 24 September (morning)

Put to him as 1 to 5 (this list's decisions 2, 5, 9, 10 and 13). His words:
