Step 1 of the streamline method in `streamline-plan.md`, for one topic: the six
subject files, the two files that say how a subject file is made, and how the
designers, the reviewer and the programs read them. Nothing in the plugin was
changed to make it.

**Snapshot.** The plugin's working tree on the night of 23 to 24 September
2026: lesson-v4 4.2.287 (commit `79426973`) with the success-criteria change
(4.2.288) uncommitted on top; the six subject files, the authoring guide and the
skill were unchanged when the check was folded in on 24 September. Quotes are
taken from that working tree, not from a commit, and line numbers are the line
in the working tree where the quote starts. Every «quoted» passage is the file's
own words; `streamline-tools/check-ledger-quotes.py` confirms each one exists in
the file named in its Where column.

**What counts as this topic.** The six subject files, whole
(`subject-maths.md`, `subject-history.md`, `subject-geography.md`,
`subject-science.md`, `subject-re.md`, `subject-pshe.md`); the authoring guide
(`authoring-subject-files.md`) and the make-subject-file skill, which say what a
subject file holds and how one is written; every line elsewhere that tells an
agent how to find or read a subject file, or points into one; and what the
programs read from them (the review packet's subject list, its card and skipped
section, the validator's maths name, its idea check and the message that quotes
the maths file).

**What belongs to a neighbouring topic.** The subject files are where the
earlier topics reached furthest: 201 rows here are held by an earlier list and
marked shared with that row's ID (assumed knowledge, the rhythm, quick checks,
vocabulary, success criteria, starters, worksheets). IDs marked "pinned" are in
that topic's pin file and were matched to today's text. The reviewer (`RV-`),
routes (`RT-`), starters (`SA-`) and the rest of preferences (`PF-`) lists were
built the same night and are cited by ID where they hold a row; the voice guide
(`VG-`) was built in parallel. The worksheet designer's own helper guidance per
subject (`worksheet-helpers/maths.md`, `science.md`) is also called "the subject
files" in one place, but it is the worksheets topic's, not this one.

**Who reads what.** The lesson designer reads its subject's file whole at the
start and again after the structure is chosen (A04, A05; maths asks it to skip
the Greater Depth section, D54). The reviewer reads the whole file, bar maths's
Greater Depth section, through its reading card (A17, A51, A55, A56). The
adaptation designer reads the whole file (A22). The worksheet designer is told
not to (A29). No other agent reads a subject file: the slide designer, the
decorator, the picture stage and the wall and stick-in designers meet its rules
only through the design. A lesson reads exactly one subject file, its own (A05),
so a rule written once in each subject file costs each lesson one read.
