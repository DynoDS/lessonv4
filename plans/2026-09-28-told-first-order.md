# Telling the lesson before choosing its parts: overnight test (27 to 28 September 2026)

Status: tested on Claude only (Daniel: "never test on codex"), not integrated, not committed. Waiting for his yes. Morning report: https://claude.ai/artifact/VEVQkxnSs1qXDiSG7DFwqZ

## Why

The lesson designer decided the parts (starter, word cards, representations, rhythm, criteria, apply, worksheet) before it told the lesson, so the telling was written to fit parts already fixed. The amount was only felt once told, too late, and parts felt bolted on (word cards from a plan's list rather than from the words the story had to explain). Daniel, 27 Sept: "I'm just wondering whether you think that's the most effective order."
