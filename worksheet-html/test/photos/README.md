# Test photographs

Real photographs, so the labelled-diagram helper can be exercised and looked at
with the kind of picture a lesson actually uses rather than a drawing.

`sunflower.jpg` — Unsplash, free to use. A single plant with its petals, flower
head, stem and leaves all clearly separable, which is what makes it a labelling
subject at all. Most flower photographs are not: the first two tried here were a
field of poppies and a cactus in a mug, and neither has parts a child could be
asked to name.

That is the failure the `diagram-anchor` agent exists to catch. A designer
places the dots as percentages BEFORE the photo is sourced, so a dot can land on
a blank sky or on a feature this shot does not contain. The agent looks at the
real picture and moves them.
